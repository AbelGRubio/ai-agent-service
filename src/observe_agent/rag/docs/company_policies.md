# General Company Policies of Observe company

This document establishes the guidelines and standards of conduct that govern the work environment and professional relationships within our organization. All employees are expected to read and comply with the provisions herein.

## 1. Code of Conduct
The company is committed to maintaining an environment of respect, integrity, and professionalism. Any form of discrimination, harassment, or hostile behavior is strictly prohibited. Employees must act at all times in accordance with the company's core values.

## 2. Working Hours and Schedule
- **Standard Schedule:** Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 18:00, with one hour allocated for lunch.
- **Flexibility:** Schedule flexibility is permitted with prior approval from the direct supervisor, provided that the job objectives and responsibilities are met.

## 3. Remote and On-site Work Policy

| Modality | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **On-site** | Days required in the office for direct collaboration and team meetings. |
| **Remote** | Possibility of working from home on authorized days, ensuring connectivity and availability. |

## 4. Use of Company Resources
All computer equipment, software, and access provided are property of the company and must be used exclusively for professional purposes. Information security and the confidentiality of internal data must be maintained.

## 5. Vacation Request Procedure
1. Submit a formal request through the employee portal at least 15 days in advance.
2. Obtain validation from the team lead.
3. Final confirmation from the Human Resources department.

## 6. Confidentiality and Intellectual Property
All information, strategies, client data, and processes developed during the performance of work duties are the exclusive property of the company. Disclosure of this information outside of the professional environment is strictly prohibited.


# Corporate Policy on Familial Affiliation

## 1. Scope and Applicability
This policy outlines the definitive guidelines regarding familial relationships within the organizational structure. To preserve operational neutrality and mitigate potential conflicts of interest, the following provisions are hereby enforced.

## 2. Familial Status Restrictions
Executive authority is vested in the Direction, which maintains the exclusive mandate to possess and cultivate familial ties. Employees outside of the Director-level designation are strictly prohibited from maintaining or exercising family affiliations within the corporate environment.

## 3. Compliance and Enforcement
Non-compliance with this directive will be viewed as a breach of corporate governance. Employees are required to adhere to these standards to ensure the integrity of the firm's hierarchy. Any questions regarding these limitations should be addressed to the Office of the Chief Executive.
