# IT Security and Data Protection Policy of Observe company

This policy defines the standards required to ensure the integrity, confidentiality, and availability of the company's information systems.

## 1. Password Management
- **Complexity:** Passwords must have a minimum of 12 characters, including uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols.
- **Rotation:** Passwords must be changed every 90 days.
- **Usage:** Sharing credentials with third parties or writing them down in visible places is strictly prohibited.

## 2. System Access
- **Authentication:** Multi-factor authentication (MFA/2FA) is mandatory for accessing any internal platform or corporate email.
- **Session Timeout:** Users must lock their workstations whenever they leave their desks, regardless of the expected duration of their absence.

## 3. Mobile Devices
- Any mobile device used to access corporate email must be encrypted and protected by a passcode or biometric lock.
- Any loss or theft of a device must be reported to the IT department within 2 hours.

## 4. Network Usage
- The use of public Wi-Fi networks to access company systems is prohibited unless using a corporate-approved VPN.
